# v1.2 Order Form - source text (verbatim from runbook Appendix A, 30 Jul 2026 attachment)

**Header:** Order Form. Effective date / Order no.
"This Order Form is entered into under the General Subscription Terms of Business found at: https://www.centellic.com/general-subscription-terms-of-business/ and the applicable Product Schedule(s), all of which together constitute the Agreement. In the event of conflict, this Order Form prevails, then the applicable Product Schedule, then the General Terms."

**1. Parties.** Company (Centellic): legal entity name, registration number, registered office. Customer: legal entity name, registration number, VAT/GST/Sales Tax No. (if applicable) [Number]/[N/A], registered/principal address.

**2. Customer contacts.** Rows: Main/commercial; Billing/invoice; Legal/notices. Columns: role, name and title, email/phone.

**3. Products, fees and term.** Columns: Product Description, License Model, Annual fee (excl. tax), Currency, Start date, End date. Licence models:
- "Authorised Users - Named individuals as listed in Annex A. Number of authorised users: ___"
- "Limited Access - Up to ___ authorised users (as defined in the General Terms)."
- "Benefiting Group - All individuals within the following group: [__]"
- "Enterprise-Wide Access - All current staff of the Customer and its Affiliates. Customer warrants approximately ___ staff at the Effective Date and will notify the Company promptly if headcount increases by more than 5%."
Total annual fee row. Then: "All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes (VAT / GST). The Customer is responsible for all applicable withholding taxes."

**4. Fees, renewal and payment.** Initial term [TBC]. Billing frequency: Annual (full year upfront). Payment due: Immediate on receipt of valid invoice. PO number (if required).

**6. Governing law and jurisdiction.** English law (courts of England and Wales) or New York law (courts of New York County, NY). Rendered as resolved text in the PDF, never as checkboxes.

**7. Special Terms.** "Any agreed variations. If none, write 'None'."

**8. Execution.** "By signing below, the Customer confirms it has read, understood and agrees to be bound by this Order Form and the Agreement. The signatory warrants that they are duly authorised to execute this Order Form on behalf of the Customer." Signature, Name (print), Position/title, Date. Customer signature only.
