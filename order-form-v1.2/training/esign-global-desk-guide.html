eSign Global Desk Guide

Adobe Sign emails don't reliably reach mainland China, so China-bound agreements go through eSign Global instead. Same Order Form, same automation on signature — only the signing rail changes. Five steps.

When to use this route

The customer's signer is in mainland China (or their firm requires a China-accessible signing platform). Hong Kong signers on international email can usually stay on the normal Adobe route — use your judgement on the firm.

The five steps

  1. Prepare the quote exactly as normal.Approved quote, Primary Contact right, Benefiting Group descriptions filled, seats checked — everything in the Order Form Academy applies unchanged.
  2. Generate the document with the Subscription Order Form template.Generate Document on the quote, pick Subscription Order Form, keep the default name. The brand logo, legal entity, licence wording and terms all derive as usual.
  3. Create the eSignGlobal Agreement.On the quote, open the ESign Agreements tab → New. Link the Quote Document you just generated and set Contact to the person who signs. Account and Opportunity link the deal.
  4. Click Send for Signature on the agreement.The document uploads to eSign Global and the signature, name and title fields place themselves automatically. The sender view opens for a final look — review, then confirm the send. The customer gets a China-accessible signing email.
  5. They sign — you're done.The signed PDF files itself against the quote and the Customer Signed Date stamps automatically, exactly like the Adobe route. Nothing to download or upload.
Also automatic: chasing and withdrawing. Send Reminder re-prompts the signer; Cancel Agreement (with a reason) withdraws it — both are buttons on the agreement record.
One check the system won't do for you: the Adobe route's send button verifies the quote is Approved and the document came from the right template before anything leaves. The eSign Global route trusts you on those two checks — so confirm the quote is Approved and the linked document was generated with the Subscription Order Form template before you hit Send for Signature.

If something looks wrong

SymptomWhat it means & what to do
Agreement shows an ErrorThe Error field on the agreement says what failed. Most often the linked Quote Document is missing its PDF — regenerate the document, relink, send again. Still stuck: post in the CRM support channel.
Signer never received the emailCheck the Contact's email address, then use Send Reminder. eSign Global delivery inside China is reliable once the address is right.
Wrong document went outCancel Agreement with a reason, regenerate the Order Form, create a fresh agreement, send again.
Signed date didn't appear on the quoteThe stamp normally lands within a minute of signing. If it hasn't after ten, tell the CRM team — the signed PDF is safe either way.
