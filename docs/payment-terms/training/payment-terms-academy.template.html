Approving extended terms

The request, what to check on the quote, making a PO a condition, approving, rejecting with a reason, and the Director's step.

Transcript

When a rep asks for extended terms, the request lands with Credit Control. Here's the whole job.

Everything you need is on the request: the quote, the terms asked for, and the rep's justification. Open the quote for the value and the account.

On the quote, the annualised value in pounds and the number of days are right beside the terms, so you're comparing like with like, whatever the currency of the deal.

If your answer is yes, but only with a purchase order, you can make that a condition. Set PO Required to Yes on the quote before you approve. The signing flow will then refuse to complete until the customer enters their PO number. Nothing to chase after signature.

Finance · 1 min

Who approves what

The two finance approver groups, the split by quote type, and what changed for Net 30 and shorter.

Transcript

Two groups now handle every finance approval on a quote, and the split is by the kind of quote, not by brand.

Credit Control, Samantha, Leslie and Candice, with Rahul as UK backup, takes every check on new and renewal quotes: extended payment terms, bad-debtor and finance-problem accounts, big deals, special terms and card thresholds. All brands, including ALM.

Kevin, Chloe, Willie, Grace and Sherry take the same checks on amendment and reissue quotes only. They never see a new quote.

Step by step

Showing the guide for your role. Switch roles at the top of the page to see the others.

Pick the terms in Payment Information

Edit the quote and choose Payment Terms. Anything up to Net 30 saves straight away and never goes to Finance. Net 45 and Net 60 are the normal extended terms. Net 75, 90 and 120 are for genuinely exceptional cases and follow exactly the same route.

Check the deal is big enough

Extended terms need at least USD 10,000 a year of contract value. The quote works this out for you as Annual Contract Value (GBP), which is the net amount divided by the number of years in the term. Below GBP 7,500 (about USD 10,000 or EUR 8,600) the save is refused and the message says why.

This is a hard stop, not an approval. A PO does not change it. Pick Net 30 or shorter and move on.

Write the justification

On a qualifying deal the quote asks for a Payment Terms Justification before it will save. Two or three lines are enough: total contract value and length, who the customer is, and anything you know about their credit or their accounts-payable policy. This is the case Finance reads first.

After saving, look under the field: Payment Terms (Days) and Annual Contract Value (GBP) are what Credit Control sees.

Submit for approval and watch it move

Submit for approval exactly as you do today. The Approvals list on the quote shows two steps: Credit Control first, then the Director of Financial Control. Step 2 only opens once step 1 is approved. Other approvals your quote needs (discounts, legal terms) run alongside as before.

If it is rejected, read the reason and resubmit

A rejection brings the approver's comment back onto the quote. Change the terms (Net 45 instead of Net 60 is a common answer), or adjust the deal, then submit again. If Credit Control has set PO Required to Yes, the customer will have to enter a PO number when they sign.

Open the request

You get the usual approval notification. The approval record carries the rule (Payment Terms > 30 Days), the step, and a link to the quote. The rep's justification is on the quote in Payment Information.

Check the value, the days and the account

Beside Payment Terms you have Payment Terms (Days) and Annual Contract Value (GBP), already converted from USD or EUR, so every request is compared on the same basis. The account's Bad Debtor status and Account Status are in the highlights at the top of the quote.

You will never see a request under GBP 7,500 a year. The quote refuses those before they can be submitted.

Optional: make a PO a condition

If your answer is "yes, but only with a purchase order", edit the quote and set PO Required to Yes before you approve. The order form will not complete signature until the customer has entered a PO number. You have edit access to PO Required and PO Number on every quote for this purpose.

Approve or reject, with a comment

Approve from the approval record or the Approve link on the quote. Approval moves the request to the Director of Financial Control automatically. If you reject, say why in the comment: it is shown to the rep on the quote and is the only message they get.

A good rejection is one line: "Credit exposure too high for 60 days on a new client; offer Net 45, or Net 60 with a PO."

Director's step

The Director of Financial Control only sees requests Credit Control has already approved, with the same information plus Credit Control's comment. Approve or reject in the same way. The whole trail (both steps, both comments) stays on the quote.

Know the two approver groups

Every finance approval on a quote now routes to one of two public groups, split by quote type. Credit Control (Samantha Law, Leslie Perry, Candice Goodpaster, Rahul Vadgama as UK backup) takes all checks on new and renewal quotes, all brands including ALM. Finance - Amendments (Kevin, Chloe, Willie, Grace, Sherry) takes the same checks on amendment and reissue quotes only.

Change membership without a rebuild

Holiday cover, joiners and leavers are edits to the public group. No approval rule needs touching. Ask the CRM team (Kamyar Jannati) and it is a same-day change.

What no longer reaches Finance

Net 30 and shorter never create a finance approval, and quotes under GBP 7,500 a year cannot ask for extended terms at all. Around 700 requests a year that had no policy reason have gone. The other finance checks (bad debtor, finance-problem accounts, deals of USD 50,000 or more, special terms, card thresholds) are unchanged in substance and now route to the two groups above.

Thresholds and rates

The check runs on GBP 7,500 annualised. USD and EUR quotes convert at the CRM's fixed rates (USD 1.2688 and EUR 1.1497 to the pound), so USD 10,000 and EUR 8,600 are the equivalents reps see in the message. Changing the threshold or a rate is a CRM change, not a rule rewrite; raise it with Kamyar.

Questions people have asked

Answers agreed with Lina Patel, Director of Financial Control, unless stated.

The customer is small but will give us a PO. Can we do Net 45?

No. Under USD 10,000 a year (GBP 7,500 / EUR 8,600), extended terms are a hard no regardless of a PO. The quote will not save with Net 45 or longer, and there is no approval route.

Does Net 30 need approval now?

No. Net 30 and shorter are self-service on every quote and never create a finance approval. Before September, Net 15 and Net 30 quotes were generating around 700 needless finance approvals a year.

Over the threshold, is a PO always required for extended terms?

Normally but not exclusively. Credit Control decides case by case and can make a PO a condition by setting PO Required to Yes on the quote before approving. The order form then insists on a PO number at signature.

Is PO Required on the quote the same as the PO flag on the account?

No, they are two fields. The account flag is curated by invoicing on Firm and Office accounts and the order form honours it directly: if the account says PO required, the PO box is mandatory at signature whatever the quote says. The quote field is set by Credit Control as an approval condition, or by the signer's own Yes/No answer on the order form. Nothing copies one into the other.

Our quotes are in EUR or USD. Does the threshold change?

The policy threshold is the same value in every currency. The quote converts to pounds at the CRM's fixed rate and checks against GBP 7,500, which is USD 10,000 or EUR 8,600.

What about a renewal that already has Net 60 from last year?

Inherited terms are not re-checked. The checks fire when someone changes Payment Terms on the quote. If you change the renewal's terms, the normal rules apply to the new value.

When would I use Net 75, 90 or 120?

Only for a genuinely exceptional case, and expect Credit Control to push back. They follow the same route as Net 45 and Net 60: justification, then Credit Control, then the Director.

Where has the ALM Credit Control approval gone?

It has been replaced. The old ALM-only rule is retired and ALM quotes now use the same global chain as every other brand. Credit Control still sees them, just through the new rule.

My quote was rejected. Do I need to start again?

No. Read the comment on the approval, change the terms or the deal, and submit for approval again. The previous request is kept on the quote as history.

Something else needs approval on the same quote. Does this change that?

No. Discount, legal-terms and other quote approvals run as before. The payment terms chain is one more line in the same Approvals list.
